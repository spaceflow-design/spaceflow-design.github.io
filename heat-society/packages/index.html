How credits work

Activated by your first session
The validity period only starts counting from the first class you take on the package, not from the day you buy it.
Closest-to-expiry credits first
The app automatically uses the batch of credits closest to expiry first, so you never have to keep track.
Cancel 10 hours ahead and the credit returns
Cancel later than that, or miss the class, and that session's credit is not refunded.
No refunds
Credits cannot be transferred or exchanged for cash.

Before you buy

The three most common questions about credits.

Message on Zalo

I've never done Pilates. Which class should I start with?

Flow 50 or Restore 45. In both, the coach corrects each person's form. Once you feel comfortable, try Burn 60. The First Heat package is made for your first session.

How do I cancel a class?

Cancel in the app at least 10 hours before the class and the credit returns to your account automatically. Cancel later than that, or miss the class, and that session's credit is not refunded.

Do credits expire?

Yes. Each package has its own validity period, counted from the first class you take on it. The app reminds you before credits are about to expire and uses the batch closest to expiry first.
