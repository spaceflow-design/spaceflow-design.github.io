Studio

A studio built for the evenings.

The story, the method and the people who lead the classes.

We opened Heat Society because Hanoi was missing a place like this.

A place where training doesn't have to be loud to work. We took mat Pilates as the foundation, added infrared heat so the body warms from within, and kept classes small so every person is seen.

No leaderboards, no badges. Only your heart rate, your effort and a room dark enough that you never have to wonder who is watching.

The method

Every class moves through four phases, whether it's Burn, Flow or Restore. The intensity changes; the structure doesn't.

  1. 01

    Warm up with infrared

    Infrared panels warm the deep tissue before you move, so joints and muscles are ready sooner and more safely than in a room heated by air.

  2. 02

    Slow, controlled resistance

    Long holds, small ranges of motion, a focus on the deep muscles. No jumping, no impact. The breath leads the movement, not the other way round.

  3. 03

    Guided by heart rate

    The HRM strap tells you which zone you're in. The coach watches the screen to push or ease the whole room at the right moment.

  4. 04

    Cool down and recover

    The last five minutes are for breathing, stretching and letting the heat drain away slowly. You leave the room with your heart rate back to normal.

Three classes, one warm room.

Frequently asked questions

If you can't find your answer, message the studio.

Message on Zalo

I've never done Pilates. Which class should I start with?

Flow 50 or Restore 45. In both, the coach corrects each person's form. Once you feel comfortable, try Burn 60. The First Heat package is made for your first session.

How hot is the room, and is it hard to breathe?

Infrared heat warms the body directly rather than heating the air like a sauna. The room is 35 to 40 degrees depending on the class, with humidity kept under control. If you have a heart condition or are pregnant, check with your doctor first.

What should I bring?

Comfortable training clothes and a large bottle of water. The studio provides mats, towels and heart-rate straps. Showers and lockers are available.

How do I cancel a class?

Cancel in the app at least 10 hours before the class and the credit returns to your account automatically. Cancel later than that, or miss the class, and that session's credit is not refunded.

Do credits expire?

Yes. Each package has its own validity period, counted from the first class you take on it. The app reminds you before credits are about to expire and uses the batch closest to expiry first.

How does the heart-rate strap work?

You collect the strap at the front desk and wear it around your chest; your numbers show on the studio screen. After the session, The Burn appears in the app with your effort score, estimated kcal and heart-rate zones.
