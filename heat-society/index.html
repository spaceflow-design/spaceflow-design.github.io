Classes

Three classes, one warm room.

Choose by the heat you want, not by level.

All three classes take place in an infrared room at 38 to 40 degrees, with no more than 17 people, and run 45 to 60 minutes. Mats, towels and water are provided; just arrive 10 minutes early. If you are new, start with Flow or Restore before trying Burn.

Burn 60

The signature class

Mat Pilates in infrared heat, with slow, controlled resistance. A heart-rate strap is worn for the whole session and the numbers show on the studio screen. It ends with The Burn in the app.
