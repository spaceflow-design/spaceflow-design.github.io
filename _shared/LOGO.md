# Spaceflow Design logo mark

`logo-mark.svg` (bone ink, for dark grounds) and `logo-mark-dark.svg` (near-black ink, for light grounds): an orbit ring that
unwinds into one flowing line, with a sky-blue dot (#0EA5E9) where the orbit begins.

Generated 2026-10-09 with Magnific (Recraft V4.1 pro, mark only, 4 variants; GPT 2.5 wordmark variants were generated too but
not used), traced to SVG with Magnific's vectoriser, white background removed, viewBox cropped to the mark. The wordmark is set
live in DM Mono ("spaceflow" + ".design" in the accent colour) so it stays crisp and themable.
