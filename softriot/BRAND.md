# Soft Riot — fictional label for the showcase "mix" page

Placeholder brand invented for a Spaceflow Design preview. Nothing here is a real company.

## The brand
Soft Riot is a Saigon studio label that puts **knitwear** (soft, hand-feel, colour-blocked) next to **corsetry** (structure, drama, evening).
Two references were fused, and the page must not look like either:
- paloma wool (Barcelona): art-school calm, runway video as the only hero, small serif nav, vertical wordmark, lookbook photography treated like a gallery wall, projects & collaborations with artists.
- Fancì Club (Saigon): hyper-feminine drama, campaign imagery full-bleed, collection names shouted in capitals ("FW26 JOINED"), tight product grid, prices in USD, lots of product.

Soft Riot's own voice: quiet confidence, a little mischief. Sentence case, short lines, no exclamation marks. Prices in USD.
