01 / Understanding

What we heard

Your brief, in our words

KATTI is contemporary womenswear built on structured silhouettes, rich texture, and a feeling of nostalgia and mystery. It is accessible luxury: couture presence and detail, designed for a modern wardrobe rather than for one occasion.

The brand opens with four jackets and grows through a system of drops. So the website has to feel like an editorial space on launch day, and make room for every drop that follows. From your references we take the editorial pacing and photography-led pages. From GIA Studios, the easy shopping flow, with more warmth and texture than its minimal look.

02 / Approach

The approach

Custom Shopify, led by the lookbook, built for drops

  • Designed from scratch for KATTI

    Every page is designed around your brand guide, then built as a custom Shopify theme. Shopify takes care of checkout, security and hosting, so the store is dependable from the first order.

  • Led by the lookbook

    The lookbook sets the mood the way a magazine spread does, and every look leads straight to the jacket in it. Texture and detail get room to breathe, on desktop and on phones.

  • Ready for every drop

    New drops, banners and lookbook stories are sections you edit yourself in Shopify’s theme editor. Launching Drop 02 won’t need a developer.

05 / Payments

Payments & markets

As agreed

A short summary of what we agreed. Shopify Payments isn’t available to businesses registered in Vietnam, so checkout runs on two providers that are.

PayPal
Customers pay with their PayPal account.
Visa & Mastercard
Through a card provider that works with Vietnam-registered businesses, such as 2C2P (via M-Pay). Customers enter their card at checkout or on a secure payment page.
Apple Pay
Not included. As you confirmed, card payments are enough.
Currency
USD. PayPal does not support VND.
